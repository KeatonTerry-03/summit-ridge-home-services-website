Our story

About Summit Ridge

Locally owned, Springfield-based, and built around dependable help for the smaller home projects that can be surprisingly difficult to schedule.

How Summit Ridge came to be

Summit Ridge Home Services started about six years ago after recognizing how difficult it could be for Springfield homeowners to find dependable help for smaller jobs.

Large contractors often are not interested in those projects, while independent help can sometimes be difficult to schedule or communicate with. Summit Ridge was created to give everyday repairs and maintenance the attention they deserve.

Most of the company’s work has grown through referrals and local relationships throughout Springfield and nearby communities.
